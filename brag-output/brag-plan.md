# Brag Plan: Soterra

## What is this app?
An emergency-information app and phone hotline that answers **only** from official, timestamped bulletins, and says "I don't know yet" when no official source has answered.

## The angle
In a disaster, the scary part is not knowing what to trust. Soterra's whole personality is restraint: every alert names its source and time, and the honest "I don't know yet" is a feature, not an error. The video is a calm, premium product film that shows exactly that, and ends on the phone number, because the hotline needs no app.

## Hook (first 2-4 seconds)
Dark Abyss frame. Giant Outfit line: **"What's happening near you?"** Quiet, then the red alert card rises on the strongest cue.

## Key moments
- The Evacuation Warning card: "Evacuation Warning. Be ready to leave." with Issued / Now times and the official source.
- The map: zones, the fire, shelter pins arriving one by one, then the shelter card sliding up above the tab bar.
- The chat: someone asks something no official source has answered. Reply: "I don't know yet."

## Outro / punchline
"Or just call." The hotline number, "English · Español · 24/7", the lowercase soterra wordmark.

## User flow worth showing
Entry (alert) → key action (open the map, tap a shelter) → result (a sourced answer, or an honest "I don't know yet").

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film, calm under pressure
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, soft crossfades, restrained audio. Humor is absent on purpose; trust is the joke-free point.

## Format: vertical, 1080x1920 (it is a phone app)
## Duration: 24s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: Abyss `#041421`
- Accent: Sage `#86B9B0`; Slate Teal `#4C7273`
- Text: Mist `#D0D6D6`; app UI is light mode: surface `#FFFFFF`, page `#E4E9E9`, ink `#041421`
- Severity: red `#FF3B30`, yellow `#FFD60A` (alert tints as in the app)
- Display font: Outfit SemiBold (bundled in the app); body: system sans
- Strongest visual element: the severity-tinted alert card with the time strip

## Share copy (draft)
Soterra answers from official sources only, and says "I don't know yet" when nobody official has answered. App + phone line.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9` (114.84 BPM), CC BY 4.0, Sascha Ende (ende.app)
- Music treatment: starts at 0, ~0.45 volume, 0.6s fade in, fade out over the last 1.8s
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cues used: 4.23s (alert card lands), 11.60s (shelter card slides up). Beat grid 10.01 / 10.54 for the two shelter pins.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (the extraction helper from the media-use skill is not installed here; documented, not blocking)
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, low-HF-risk files only
- Audio-coupled moments: typed chat question (key ticks), pins (soft clicks), card reveals (soft impact)
- Restraint rule: nothing alarm-like. No sirens, no risers. The subject is serious.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 4.0s
"What's happening near you?" fades up word by word, holds ~2s.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: calm; music bed only
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The alert — 5.3s
Alert card rises at 4.23s (beat-locked). Chip "Severe · Warning", headline "Evacuation Warning. Be ready to leave.", area, Issued 1:45 PM / Now 2:42 PM, source line with Official tag. Caption above: "Official sources. Timestamped."
Audio intent: soft impact on landing
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The map — 5.0s
Map with zone SON-3A (red) and SON-3B (yellow), fire circle. Two shelter pins arrive on beats 10.01 and 10.54. At 11.60 (beat-locked) the rounded shelter card slides up: "Ridgeview Community Center", "500 Elm Street · Shelter · Pets in crates", "Open · 120 of 200 spaces", Directions. "Demo zones · not real boundaries" kept small.
Sequential/interaction: yes — pins one by one, then card slides up
Audio intent: gentle clicks, one soft impact
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — I don't know yet — 4.7s
Chat: question types in "Is Hillcrest Elementary open as a shelter?" (key ticks). Reply card: "I don't know yet" + "No official source has answered this yet." Caption: "No official answer? It says so."
Sequential/interaction: yes — typed text
Audio intent: tiny key ticks, one bong on reply
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Call — 5.0s
"Or just call." Number "+1 712 600 3362", "English · Español · 24/7", wordmark.
Audio intent: music resolves and fades
Transition mood: fade to dark

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat but restrained
